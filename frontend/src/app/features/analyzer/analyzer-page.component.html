What we analyze

OriginLens combines several signals to give you an informed view, never a verdict.

C2PA

Cryptographically signed content credentials showing how an image was made and edited.

EXIF & metadata

Camera, software, timestamps, and other embedded technical and personal details.

Image characteristics

Texture, color distribution, compression artifacts, and other statistical signals.

Provenance

Indicators that help establish where the image came from and how it was produced.

AI-generation signals

Probabilistic indicators of AI generation. Honest about confidence and limitations.

Reverse-image discovery

Find where else an image has appeared online (when a provider is available).

How it works

  1. Add your image. Drop a file, paste one, or submit a public URL.
  2. Inspect technical information. Format, dimensions, color mode, file size.
  3. Analyze provenance and AI signals. Metadata, C2PA, and AI-generation signals.
  4. Review evidence. See confidence, detected signals, and explicit limitations.
  5. Optionally sanitize. Clean a local copy before sharing.

Looking for one thing in particular?

Metadata

EXIF, IPTC, XMP, and other embedded fields.

AI Signals

Probabilistic detection of AI-generated content.

C2PA

Signed content credentials and provenance claims.

Reverse Search

Where else this image has appeared online.

Privacy Cleaning
