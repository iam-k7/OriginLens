Common questions

Are my files stored on the server?

No. Images and text are processed in temporary memory and discarded after the result is returned.

How accurate is the AI-signal score?

Probabilistic. The score reflects signals that are consistent with AI-generated content; it is not a definitive verdict.

What counts as one analysis?

One uploaded image or one URL. Metadata sanitization and text analysis have their own counters.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Cancellation is one click; you keep Pro until the end of the billing period.
